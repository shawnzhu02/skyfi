# Skyfi: plan (new iOS beta app)

## Context
Shawn wants a new beta app, shipped to friends over TestFlight the same way as Passby and Sift. Skyfi does three things:
- shows a 3D globe with live planes, like Flightradar24, including takeoffs and landings at airports;
- plays music: audio from any YouTube link, plus preset 24/7 lofi streams;
- plays live ATC audio (LiveATC) mixed with the music.

He chose these:
- Native SwiftUI on the Sift pipeline.
- The name "skyfi".
- Background play: YouTube and ATC audio must keep playing when he leaves the app or locks the phone.
- "Highlight the plane that's talking" comes in phase 2.

Fact behind phase 2: VHF airband radio carries no transmitter ID, so radios at airports would not help. The speaker is found by turning the speech into text, reading the callsign, and matching it to an ADS-B plane near that feed's airport. Enhanced Radar's ATC app and SkyDecode work this way. It runs on the phone, with no server and no hardware.

## Decisions
- **Repo:** `C:\Users\Shawn\Programming\skyfi`, a new private GitHub repo `shawnzhu02/skyfi`. Bundle id `com.shawnzhu.skyfi`, iOS 17+.
- **Stack:** SwiftUI plus UIKit where needed. The Xcode project is generated by XcodeGen. There is no Mac, so CI on a GitHub macOS runner builds it.
- **Copy from Sift as-is:**
  - `sift/project.yml` (structure, with no Family Controls or SHIELD flag);
  - `.github/workflows/ios.yml` (archive, then upload to TestFlight, build number = `$GITHUB_RUN_NUMBER`);
  - `.github/workflows/beta.yml` and `scripts/asc_beta.py` (the Friends group, Beta App Review, the public link).

  `asc_beta.py` is not tied to Sift, so only the bundle id and app id change.
- **YouTube with background play:** find the audio stream on the phone with the SPM package `YouTubeKit` (alexeichhorn), using its local extraction mode.
  - The request goes out from the phone's own IP, which avoids the bot blocks YouTube puts on datacenter IPs.
  - `AVPlayer` plays the audio-only m4a stream. A live stream plays as an HLS manifest, which `AVPlayer` supports natively.
  - Fallback, only if on-device extraction breaks: a yt-dlp proxy on the Oracle box, deployed like Passby's `services/api` and `deploy/deploy.sh` (Hono, systemd, a Caddy block). Not built in beta 1.
- **Map:** `MKMapView` inside `UIViewRepresentable`, with `.hybridFlyover` / `.satelliteFlyover`. Zoomed out, MapKit draws a real globe.
  - Use MKMapView, not SwiftUI `Map`, because SwiftUI `Map` slows down with hundreds of annotations.
  - Annotation views are reused, and a level-of-detail cap keeps it to about 1,500 planes per viewport.
- **ADS-B:** airplanes.live `/v2/point/{lat}/{lon}/{nm}`, with adsb.lol `/v2/point/...` as fallback. Both are free, non-commercial and need no key.
  - Poll about every 5 s, staying under the limit of about 1 request per second.
  - The query covers the viewport center with a radius of at most 250 nm.
  - Between polls, planes move by dead reckoning (track and ground speed), which gives the smooth Flightradar24-style motion.
  - The app shows an attribution line.
- **Airports and runways:** the OurAirports CSV, cut down by a script to large and medium airports plus their runways and bundled as JSON.
- **Takeoff and landing detection:** watch each plane's `alt_baro == "ground"` change state within 3 nm of an airport.
  - Match the heading to a runway end.
  - Produce events such as "DAL234 departed KBOS 22R" for the airport's activity feed.
  - Planes on approach (descending and aligned with a runway) get a highlight.
- **ATC:** a curated `feeds.json` of LiveATC mount URLs (`http://d.liveatc.net/<mount>`) for about 40 major airports, each with its lat/lon.
  - It is played by `AVPlayer`.
  - It needs an ATS exception for `liveatc.net`, because the streams are HTTP.
  - The list can be updated remotely the way Sift updates `filters.json` (signed JSON in a public repo), so a dead mount gets fixed without a new build.
- **Mixer:** two players, music and ATC, each with its own volume and mute, plus a crossfade slider.
  - The audio session uses category `.playback`. Info.plist sets `UIBackgroundModes: [audio]`.
  - Lock screen controls use `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter` and `MPRemoteCommandCenter`.
  - The mixer handles interruptions (calls, Siri) and route changes (AirPods) and resumes afterwards.
- **Lofi presets:** a remote `presets.json`, because live YouTube IDs change.
  - It starts with Lofi Girl live (`jfKfPfyJRdk`) and the synthwave live (`4xDzrJKXOOY`). IDs are checked when building.
  - It adds 2-3 lofi radio streams that are not on YouTube (direct Icecast/HLS) as the reliable fallback.

## Phase 1: beta 1 (globe, traffic, mixer, background play)
1. **Scaffold:** `project.yml`, `Skyfi/SkyfiApp.swift`, the copied CI workflows, and `SETUP.md` (the Sift first-time App Store Connect steps, adapted).
2. **Traffic:**
   - `Traffic/ADSBClient.swift` (decode, fallback provider, backoff on 429);
   - `Traffic/TrafficStore.swift` (`@Observable`; merges by ICAO hex, interpolates, drops planes after 60 s stale, emits events).
3. **Airports:** `scripts/build_airports.py` (OurAirports to `Resources/airports.json`) and `Airports/AirportIndex.swift` (finds the nearest airport and runway end by heading).
4. **Globe:**
   - `Globe/GlobeView.swift` (MKMapView wrapper; annotation diffing; drives the poll region);
   - `Globe/PlaneAnnotationView.swift` (plane glyph rotated to track, callsign label at closer zoom);
   - airport pins with a departures and arrivals sheet (`UI/AirportSheet.swift`);
   - tapping a plane opens a card (callsign, type, altitude, speed, route if known).
5. **Audio:**
   - `Audio/Mixer.swift` (the 2 players, session, now playing, remote commands, interruptions);
   - `Audio/YouTubeSource.swift` (YouTubeKit; picks the best audio-only stream; handles live HLS);
   - `Audio/Presets.swift` and `ATC/Feeds.swift` (remote JSON with a bundled fallback);
   - `UI/MixerSheet.swift` (paste a YouTube link, preset grid, ATC picker sorted by distance from the map center, sliders).
6. **Connect map and ATC:** tapping an airport that has a feed offers "Tune tower". The globe marks the tuned airport.
7. **Tests:** `SkyfiTests/` covering ADS-B decoding, the interpolation math, takeoff and landing detection on recorded snapshots, and runway matching. CI adds an `xcodebuild test` step on the simulator.

## Phase 2: beta 2 ("who is talking")
- `ATC/Transcriber.swift`:
  - an `MTAudioProcessingTap` on the ATC `AVPlayerItem` sends buffers to on-device speech recognition;
  - `SpeechAnalyzer` on iOS 26+, `SFSpeechRecognizer` with `requiresOnDeviceRecognition` on 17-25;
  - an RMS gate splits the audio into separate transmissions.
- `ATC/CallsignParser.swift` turns speech into callsigns, using:
  - airline radio callsigns to ICAO codes ("Speedbird" = BAW, "Delta" = DAL), from a bundled `telephony.json`;
  - spoken numbers ("two three four", "niner");
  - NATO letters for N-numbers.

  Unit tests use about 50 real transcript lines.
- **Matching:** compare the parsed callsign with planes within about 60 nm of the feed's airport (the `flight` field), allowing near-misses (one changed digit).
- **On the map:** the matched plane glows and shows a live transcript bubble. A transcript strip lists recent transmissions; tapping one centers the map on that plane.
- **Ducking:** music dips while an ATC transmission is open.

## Risks to keep visible
- **YouTube:** extracting audio breaks YouTube's terms. Beta App Review may reject the external (public link) build. Internal testers skip that review.
- **LiveATC:** its terms say non-commercial only, and the site reportedly bans third-party use of its streams. A small friends beta is a grey area. A public App Store release needs their written permission.
- **ADS-B APIs:** they are non-commercial and rate limits may change. Two providers plus backoff cover this.
- **CI minutes:** each CI build costs about 10 of the roughly 200 free macOS minutes a month, so batch pushes. `ios.yml` already skips pushes that only change docs or scripts.

## Verification
- CI: `xcodegen generate`, `xcodebuild build` and `xcodebuild test` must pass on the macOS runner. No local Swift build is possible on Windows.
- Scripts: run `scripts/build_airports.py` locally; check the airport count and that the KBOS runways are present.
- Feed and preset check: a script sends a HEAD request to every LiveATC mount and checks every preset YouTube ID, and reports any that are dead.
- Device checklist on internal TestFlight (his phone):
  - the globe spins zoomed out and planes move smoothly near BOS;
  - a takeoff from KBOS shows up in the feed with the right runway;
  - Lofi Girl, a pasted YouTube link and KBOS tower all play mixed;
  - audio keeps going with the app in the background and the phone locked;
  - lock screen play/pause works;
  - audio resumes after a phone call.
- Phase 2: the parser unit tests pass; on the device, a plane is highlighted within about 3 s of its callsign being spoken on the KBOS feed.
